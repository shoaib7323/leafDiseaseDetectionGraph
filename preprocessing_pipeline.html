Pipeline Specifications & Methodologies

Background Extraction: AI-based subject matting (rembg) is utilized to ensure precise isolation of the leaf disease features, mitigating background noise completely.

Normalization Formula: Pnorm = Porig / 255.0 (Rescaling RGB arrays)

Data Augmentation Parameters (Keras ImageDataGenerator):

  • Rotation Range: ±20 degrees
  • Flipping: Horizontal & Vertical Enabled
  • Shear & Zoom Range: 0.15 / 15%
  • Fill Mode: nearest (resolves boundary artifacts)
